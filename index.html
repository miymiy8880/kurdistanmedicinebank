Who We Are

A bridge between full shelves and empty ones.

Kurdistan Medicine Bank exists because the region's shortage of medicine has never really been a shortage — it's a distribution problem. Surplus and donated medicine sits in warehouses abroad while clinics in Kurdistan run short of the basics: insulin, antibiotics, blood pressure medication, and asthma inhalers.

We built the logistics, customs clearing, and clinic relationships to close that gap — sourcing medicine, verifying quality, and getting it directly into the hands of healthcare providers.

"We don't run a single central pharmacy. We keep a hundred and twenty local ones stocked."
What We Do

Four ways we deliver medicine to those in need.

Prescription Access

We supply chronic-care medication — for diabetes, hypertension, and heart disease — to partner pharmacies at no cost to the patient.

Clinic Partnerships

We stock rural and refugee-camp clinics directly, matching inventory to what each clinic actually treats.

Emergency Relief

When displacement or localized crises cut off supply lines, we run rapid shipments of trauma and first-response medicine.

Chronic Disease Support

We help patients with long-term conditions stay on regular treatment routines instead of rationing daily dosage.
